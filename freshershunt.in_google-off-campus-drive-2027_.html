Google Off Campus Drive 2027 – full job details

The Google Off Campus Drive 2027 listing covers one of the best-known entry routes into product management in India: the Associate Product Manager programme for university graduates. The opening is in Bengaluru and sits at Google’s early-career level. New APMs are not locked into a team on day one. Google matches each hire with the team that best fits their interests and strengths.

Applications opened on 30 October 2026 and must be submitted before the end of the day on 24 November 2026. The cohort joins between April and August 2027, and every applicant hears back by the end of spring 2027. The listing does not publish a salary, a batch year or a CGPA cut-off, so treat claims about those elsewhere as unconfirmed.

Internship experience is a minimum requirement. Google places internship or teaching assistant experience in product management, software development or a similar technical field under its minimum qualifications, not the preferred list. Without either, a shortlist is unlikely.

Google Off Campus Drive 2027 applications close on 24 November 2026. Google does not name a time zone for its end-of-day cut-off, so submit by the evening of 23 November IST to be safe. The form also asks for an English transcript (official or unofficial).

What you will do in the Google Off Campus Drive 2027 role

  • Study markets and user needs closely before shaping a product or feature.
  • Ship products and features, track how they perform and improve them in short cycles.
  • Work with engineering, marketing, legal and UX teams through the full build cycle.
  • Solve problems with colleagues across regions, product areas and functions.
  • Position and package products around what users need.

Google Off Campus Drive 2027 eligibility

Here is the minimum bar Google lists for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027 APM opening:

  • Currently studying in, or graduated from, a degree in product management, computer science, engineering, data science, mathematics, statistics or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Internship or teaching assistant experience in product management, software development or a similar technical area.
  • Experience driving entrepreneurial projects or outreach inside an organisation while building cross-functional relationships.
  • Experience giving technical presentations, gained through internships, coursework or extracurricular activities.

Preferred, not mandatory: product development methods, AI/ML used in an internship or capstone project, skills in programming, data analysis, business modelling, pricing or design, availability to join between April and August 2027, and fluent English.

Not stated by Google: batch year, minimum CGPA, backlog rules and bond terms. Final-year students and recent graduates both appear to be in scope.

Google Off Campus Drive 2027 expected salary

Google has not disclosed the pay for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027 APM role. Levels.fyi shows an average total compensation of about ₹31.6 LPA for Associate Product Manager 1 (APM1) at Google in India, with a 10th–90th percentile spread of roughly ₹11.3–57.4 LPA. AmbitionBox shows ₹14–66 LPA for Google product managers across all levels (199 reports). Both are reported estimates, not Google’s offer, so ask the recruiter for a written break-up.

Google Off Campus Drive 2027 selection process

Google has not published the selection rounds for this opening. Use the plan below as a preparation guide only. It reflects how product management hiring usually runs, not a confirmed schedule for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027.

  1. Resume and transcript screening against the minimum qualifications.
  2. A recruiter conversation about your background, availability and interests.
  3. Product sense interviews: design or improve a product for a given group of users.
  4. Analytical and estimation questions, including metrics and trade-offs.
  5. Behavioural questions on leadership, teamwork and handling ambiguity.

Documents required for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027

  • Updated resume or CV (no cover letter needed). Build a clean one with the Freshershunt Resume Builder.
  • Official or unofficial transcript in English, uploaded in the Higher education section.
  • A Google Careers account to submit and track your application.

Preparation tips for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027

  • Pick two apps you use daily and write a one-page note on a problem each has, who it hurts and how you would fix it.
  • Practise metrics questions: how would you measure a feature’s success, and what would you check if a key number dropped?
  • Revise basic system ideas such as APIs, databases and latency so you can talk comfortably with engineers.
  • Prepare three short stories: leading a project, settling a disagreement and learning from a failure.
  • Use the free Freshershunt career tools and polish your opening with this self-introduction guide.

How to apply for the Google Off Campus Drive 2027

  1. Open the official job page: Associate Product Manager, University Graduate, 2027 Start on Google Careers.
  2. Sign in or create a Google Careers account and click Apply.
  3. Attach your updated resume in the Resume section; skip the cover letter.
  4. Under Higher education, choose “Now attending or Graduated” and upload your transcript in English.
  5. Check every field and submit before the end of the day on 24 November 2026.

About Google

Google, part of Alphabet Inc., builds Search, Android, Chrome, Maps, YouTube and Google Cloud. Its mission is to organise the world’s information and make it universally accessible and useful. In India it has offices in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Gurugram, and the Google Off Campus Drive 2027 APM programme is a key entry point into product management.
