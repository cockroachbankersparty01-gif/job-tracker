Important: please read

  • JOB Alert is not a government app. It is made by an independent developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the Government of India, any State or UT government, ministry, department, PSU, bank, recruitment board or commission.
  • You cannot apply through this app. It only tells you about openings. Every application, fee payment, admit card and result is on the official website, which each listing links to.
  • Information comes from official sources such as recruiting organisations' websites, their published notifications and Employment News. Always confirm dates, eligibility, age limits and fees in the official notification. If anything here differs from it, the official notification is correct.
  • We never ask for money. Ignore anyone who asks for payment for a government job, "confirmed selection" or a "setting".

Source of information

Vacancies, exam dates, admit cards, answer keys and results are collected from the official websites and published notifications of the recruiting organisations, for example:

  • Union Public Service Commission – upsc.gov.in
  • Staff Selection Commission – ssc.gov.in
  • Institute of Banking Personnel Selection – ibps.in
  • Indian Railways / Railway Recruitment Boards – indianrailways.gov.in
  • Employment News (Govt. of India) – employmentnews.gov.in
  • National Career Service – ncs.gov.in
  • State Public Service Commissions, State Subordinate Service Selection Boards, police recruitment boards, PSUs and banks – their own official websites

Each listing has a link to the official website or notification. The "Official notification" button opens the recruiting organisation's own notification (usually a PDF). Where we haven't yet found the direct file, a "Notification details" button opens a job-news page that links to it, so you know it isn't the official site.

Accuracy

We check listings carefully, but dates and vacancy numbers can change or contain mistakes. The app is provided for information only, without any warranty. We are not responsible for any loss caused by relying on it; always verify on the official website before acting.

Names and symbols

Organisation names are used only to identify who is recruiting. The app does not use the State Emblem of India or any government logo, and nothing in it is an official communication.

Advertisements

The Android app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Ads come from third parties and are not endorsed by JOB Alert or by any government body. Ads never ask you to apply for a job through them.

Privacy (summary)

  • No sign-up or account. Your qualification, sector, experience and state choices are stored only on your phone and never sent to us.
  • The app downloads job data from GitHub, which, like any web server, receives your IP address.
  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • To delete everything the app stores, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
