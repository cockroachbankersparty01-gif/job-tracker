# Privacy Policy – JOB Alert

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026

JOB Alert ("the app", "we") is an independent app that shows Indian government job openings, exam dates, admit cards, answer keys and results collected from official recruitment websites. It is **not a government app** and is not affiliated with any government body. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It applies to the JOB Alert Android app and its web version.

## 1. Summary
- There is **no sign-up, login or account**.
- We **do not collect** your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
- The choices you make in the app (qualification, job sector, experience and state) are **stored only on your phone**.
- The Android app shows **ads from Google AdMob**, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads (section 4).

## 2. Information stored on your phone
Your profile choices (qualification, job sector, experience and state), the fact that you have seen the disclaimer, and the time the app last checked for new listings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Downloading listings
Every time you open the app it downloads the latest listings and app screens from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), operated by GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic request information when it sends the files. We do not receive or store this information. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:
- device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- app interactions with ads (for example, ad views and taps);
- diagnostics and app performance data (for example, crash logs and load times).

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where the law requires it (for example for users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## 5. Links to other websites
Listings link to the official websites of recruiting organisations and, occasionally, job-news websites that reproduce official notices. Those sites open in your browser and have their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for them.

## 6. Children
The app is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 7. Sharing, sale and retention
We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not collect any. Data stored on your phone stays there until you clear it. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

## 8. Your rights
Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask about, correct or erase personal data processed about you, and raise a grievance. Since we hold no personal data on our servers, most requests are handled on your device (section 2) or through Google's ad settings (section 4). For anything else, contact us (section 10) and we will respond within 30 days.

## 9. Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
