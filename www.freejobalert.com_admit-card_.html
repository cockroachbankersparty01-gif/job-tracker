Admit Card 2026 - Hall Tickets & Call Letters for Government Exams

An admit card is the most important document a candidate needs on exam day. Every recruitment body - from the Staff Selection Commission and UPSC to the Railway Recruitment Boards, IBPS, SBI, RBI, and state public service commissions - releases admit cards online a few days before the examination. On this page you will find the latest admit cards and hall tickets 2026 with direct download links, release dates, and exam dates for each notification.

How to Download Your Admit Card Online

Downloading a hall ticket follows the same basic steps for almost every exam. Visit the official website of the conducting authority, open the admit card or candidate login section, and enter your registration number or roll number along with your date of birth or password. Verify all the printed details, then download and take a clear printout. We provide the direct admit card download link for each exam in the table above so you can reach the correct page quickly.

Details to Check on Your Hall Ticket

As soon as you download your admit card, check that your name, roll number, photograph, and signature are correct, and note the exam date, reporting time, and the full address of the exam centre. Also read the instructions printed on the call letter carefully, as they list the documents to carry and the items banned inside the hall. If any detail is wrong, contact the conducting authority immediately for correction.

Documents to Carry on Exam Day

On the day of the exam, carry a printed copy of your admit card along with an original government photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, or driving licence. Some exams also ask for a passport-size photograph or a signed self-declaration form. Reach the centre before the reporting time, as candidates are usually not allowed entry after the gate-closing time mentioned on the hall ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an admit card or hall ticket?
An admit card (also called a hall ticket or call letter) is an official document issued by the exam-conducting authority that permits a candidate to appear for an examination. It contains the exam date, time, venue, roll number, and candidate details. Carrying a valid admit card is mandatory for entry to the exam hall.

4. When is the admit card usually released?
Most admit cards are released 7 to 15 days before the exam date. The exact date is announced by the conducting authority, and we update this page as soon as call letters go live.

5. What if I forget my registration number or password?
Use the Forgot Registration Number or Forgot Password option on the login page and recover your credentials using your registered email, mobile number, or date of birth. Contact the official helpdesk if you still cannot access your admit card.

6. Can I appear for the exam without an admit card?
No. Candidates are not allowed into the exam hall without a valid admit card. You must carry a printed hall ticket along with a government photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, or voter ID.

7. Is the admit card sent by post?
No, admit cards are not posted. They are released online only and must be downloaded from the official website and printed by the candidate before the exam.

8. What documents should I carry with the admit card?
Carry the printed admit card, an original government photo ID, a passport-size photograph if asked, and any documents specified in the exam instructions. Some exams also require a signed self-declaration form.

9. What should I do if there is a mistake on my admit card?
If you find an error in your name, photo, centre, or other details, contact the conducting authority through the official helpline or email immediately for correction before the exam day.
