Frequently asked questions

What is an off-campus drive?

An off-campus drive is a company hiring event open to students or graduates outside a single college’s placement cell. Eligibility, dates, and apply links come from the employer.

Can 2025 pass-outs apply?

Often yes for fresher drives, but each employer sets its own batch rules. Read the listing and the company careers page before you apply.

Is there a fee to apply for off-campus drives?

Genuine drives are free to apply. Never pay a registration fee, training fee, or share OTPs to “confirm” an offer.
