# JOB Alert

A mobile-friendly web app for Indian government job openings, admit cards, answer keys, results and an exam calendar, filtered by the candidate's qualification, preferred sector and experience.

- `index.html`: the app
- `data.json`: listings, refreshed daily
- Open the GitHub Pages link in any phone browser; no login needed. Pull down to refresh.

Listings are compiled from public recruitment notices and job-news sites. Always confirm dates and eligibility in the official notification before applying.

## PRO Job Alert (private jobs)

A second app in `pro/` for private-sector jobs, off-campus drives and internships for B.Tech/BE, MCA, MBA, B.Com/CA, B.Pharm, B.Sc and other professional degrees.

- `pro/index.html`: the app · `pro/data.json`: listings, refreshed daily
- Android app: `android-pro/` · APK: Releases → `pro-apk-latest` → `PROJobAlert.apk`
